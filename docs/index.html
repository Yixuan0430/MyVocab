Support & FAQ / 支持与常见问题

  • Importing decks: Import local .apkg (including modern .anki21b), .csv, or .txt files from the Files app, AirDrop, or browse community-shared decks via the built-in AnkiWeb catalog.
  • iCloud Sync: Sign in with the same Apple Account and enable iCloud Drive on your iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Your decks, review history, and study preferences sync privately via Apple CloudKit.
  • Pronunciation: MyVocab uses Apple system text-to-speech (AVSpeechSynthesizer) automatically matched to each deck's language. You can download enhanced voices in iOS Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices.
  • Contact: For bug reports or feature requests, open an issue on GitHub Issues or email yixuancn2026@gmail.com.
