Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026 · Back to Support

MyVocab is designed with privacy first. The developer does not operate backend tracking servers, display ads, or collect personal data.

1. Data Stored Locally & in Your Private iCloud

Your imported vocabulary decks, spaced-repetition review logs, and study settings are stored locally on your device. If iCloud is enabled on your Apple Account, this data syncs exclusively through Apple's private CloudKit database (iCloud.com.yixuanwu.lexikos) and iCloud Key-Value Store, accessible only to your own signed-in Apple devices. The developer cannot view or access your private iCloud data.

2. Optional Network Requests

When you choose to inspect or download a community-shared deck from the in-app AnkiWeb catalog or by pasting an AnkiWeb shared deck link, the app connects directly to ankiweb.net to fetch deck metadata, sample previews, and deck packages. No MyVocab user identifiers or analytics are sent.

3. Tracking, Analytics & Third-Party SDKs

MyVocab contains no advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics, and does not track you across apps or websites.

4. Data Deletion

You can delete any deck directly inside the app (which also removes its synced record from your private iCloud container) or uninstall the app and manage iCloud storage in iOS Settings.

5. Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact yixuancn2026@gmail.com.

隐私政策

MyVocab 坚持本地优先与零追踪设计，开发者不收集任何个人身份信息，不含广告或第三方统计 SDK。

你的词库、复习记录与学习偏好仅保存于你的设备本地；启用 iCloud 时，数据仅通过 Apple 私有 CloudKit 容器在你的个人设备间同步，开发者无法读取。仅当你主动浏览、预览或下载 AnkiWeb 公开共享词库时，应用才会直接向 ankiweb.net 请求公开词库数据。

如有隐私相关疑问，请联系 yixuancn2026@gmail.com。
